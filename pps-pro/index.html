PPS-Pro dosing

PPS-Pro doses a small amount every day, aiming to replace roughly what plants use rather than flood the tank. Levels stay low and steady, water changes can be smaller, and it suits lower-light or lower-CO2 tanks. The trade-off: it's lean on purpose, so you watch the plants and adjust. You mix two stock bottles once; this tells you what goes in them and how many mL to dose each day.

Water & bottles

Leave the daily dose blank for the PPS-Pro standard: 1 mL per 10 US gal. Pick a round number that suits your pump or syringe.

Where levels top out

If plants took up nothing, a weekly change of 30% holds each nutrient below this ceiling.3 PPS-Pro assumes plants use most of it, so real levels should sit far lower.

How this is calculated 
Standard recipe (reference only; targets are editable):
  Macro bottle: 500 mL + 58 g KNO3 + 4 g KH2PO4 + 33 g MgSO4·7H2O
  Micro bottle: 500 mL + 8 g CSM+B
  Dose: 1 mL of each per 10 US gal (37.854 L) per day

Daily ppm the recipe delivers = g/500 mL × 1000 mg/g × 1 mL ÷ 37.854 L × fraction
  NO3  58 g × 0.61328 → 1.879     PO4 4 g × 0.69788 → 0.147
  Mg   33 g × 0.09861 → 0.172     Fe  8 g × 0.0653  → 0.0276
  K    58 × 0.38672 + 4 × 0.28731 → 1.246

Your bottle, for any target:
  doses per bottle = bottle mL ÷ dose mL
  g in bottle = target ppm × V ÷ (fraction × 1000) × doses per bottle

Mass fractions: KNO3 NO3 0.61328 · KH2PO4 PO4 0.69788 · K2SO4 K 0.44875
                MgSO4·7H2O (246.466 g/mol) Mg 0.09861

Ceiling with no uptake: D = 7 × daily ppm, f = weekly change fraction
  before WC = D/f;  after WC = D(1 − f)/f

Notes

  1. Recipe provenance. 58 g KNO3, 4 g KH2PO4 and 33 g MgSO4·7H2O per 500 mL, plus 8 g CSM+B per 500 mL, dosed at 1 mL per 10 US gal, is the PPS-Pro recipe as it is widely circulated. We haven't been able to check it against the original author's page. If your source differs, type its daily ppm into the targets; everything else follows.
  2. Solubility. Limits used are single-salt figures at 20 °C (g per 100 mL water): KNO3 31.6, KH2PO4 22.6, K2SO4 11.1, MgSO4·7H2O 71. Several potassium salts in one bottle hold less than each would alone, so we warn at 60% of any limit. Cold storage lowers it further.
  3. The ceiling ignores plant uptake, so it's the most that can build up, not a prediction. It assumes one water change of the same size every week.
  4. Trace mix. Fe % defaults to the Plantex CSM+B label (6.53%). A different mix needs its own iron %.
